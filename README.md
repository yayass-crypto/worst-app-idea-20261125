Entry for the make.fun bounty
[The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23)
— one sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching the worst and most
useless app imaginable. Prize $2 USDC, 8 places (1st $0.64), free entry.

## The pitch

> SockSync is an app that pairs your single socks with strangers' single socks for $4.99 a month, and texts you every morning to remind you that you still have no matching pair.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and nothing
else. It checks the gate itself first: 1–240 characters, UTF-8, not only
white space. The pitch sounds like a real founder: a product name, a
monthly price, a matching mechanism — for a problem nobody has, and the
app then charges you to be reminded the problem is still there.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
